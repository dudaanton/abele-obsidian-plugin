# History timeline

A view for Obsidian Bases that lays your notes about history on one line of years: people from
birth to death, events as points, wars and reigns as bars, eras behind them. Pick someone to see
who lived at the same time.

## Making one

Make a base over your history notes, add a view and pick **Timeline**. Group the base by a
property, a category say, and each group becomes a row with its own colour. A group called
**Эпохи** or **Eras** is drawn behind everything as eras; **Eras group** in the view's options
names another.

The timeline reads the dates it finds: `born` and `died` for people, `start` and `end` for wars,
`date` for a battle, `period` for a whole span in one field — each note by what it has, so they
can all sit in one base. **Start** and **End** in the view's options choose other properties.

## Writing dates

Write dates as text, the way you would say them: `1564`, `26 апреля 1564`, `1560-е`, `XVI век`,
`вторая половина XVI века`, `ок. 1450`, `1450?`, `490 до н.э.` or `-490`, `now`. English works
too: `1560s`, `16th century`, `c. 1450`, `490 BC`. A range like `1440–1455` in the start means
"born somewhere between"; a vague date is drawn with a soft edge and labelled as you wrote it.

A negative year is before Christ, the way people count: `-490` is 490 BC, and there is no year
zero, so Augustus (63 BC – AD 14) lived 76 years.

**Years in "about"** in the view's options sets how far `ок. 1450` reaches, five years by default.

## Moving about

- Drag to move along the years and up and down the rows; on a phone, with one finger.
- Zoom with a pinch, Ctrl or Cmd with the wheel, or the **Millennia**, **Centuries**,
  **Decades**, **Years** buttons (**Days** appears when your notes have days).
- Type a year or a name into the field at the top and press Enter to go there.
- The strip at the bottom is all of history in the base; press or drag it to jump.
- When a row is crowded, the least-linked notes are gathered into a "+N"; press it to zoom in.
  The base's own filters are the way to narrow what is shown.

## Contemporaries

Press a bar to pick it. Everyone who lived at the same time moves to the top, one line each, with
the years they shared underlined, and the rest dims. The list beside the timeline (under it on a
phone) says how many years each lived alongside. Pick an event to see who was alive then and how
old; pick an era to see who lived in it. Press empty space or Escape to let go.

Hover over a bar to see its dates as written and how old the person was at the year under the
pointer. A note's `cover` picture is shown round on its bar and in that card.

## Opening and making notes

Double-click a bar, or Cmd or Ctrl and click, to open its note; **Open note** in the list does it
too. Right-click — or hold a finger on a phone — for **New note in** that year: it goes where the
base's own **New** button puts notes, with the year written into its start.
